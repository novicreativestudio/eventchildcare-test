Join Our Team

Qualified sitters wanted

We are building a small team of qualified sitters for weekend and evening events across Metro Vancouver. If you work in childcare and enjoy a good party, we would love to hear from you.

Who we are looking for

Childcare qualifications and hands-on experience with young children. First aid and a background check are part of our process.

How it works

Event by event, mostly weekends and evenings, about 3 to 4 hours each. The plan and the kids corner are prepared in advance. Say yes only to the dates that suit you.

How to apply

Email us a few lines about your qualifications, your experience and the areas you can travel to. No formal resume needed to start.
